1. Be respectful

  • Treat other members kindly. Disagreement is okay; harassment, bullying, threats, hate and targeted humiliation are not.
  • Do not impersonate another member or staff member.
  • Do not spam, flood the site, or deliberately disrupt the community.

2. Protect privacy

  • Never post passwords, private messages, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, school details, live location or identifying documents.
  • Do not publish another person's image or personal information without their permission.
  • Do not try to identify, track, contact or expose another member outside the site.

3. Image board content

  • Post only images you created or have permission to share. Credit the original creator when appropriate.
  • Threads, reply text, and reply images are held for staff review before becoming public.
  • Public posts are labeled Anonymous, but signing in is required and account IDs are retained privately for moderation and safety.
  • No sexual or nude content, fetish content, graphic violence, gore, self-harm imagery, or content that encourages dangerous behavior.
  • No hateful symbols, targeted harassment, doxxing, scams, malware or deceptive links.
  • Use accurate thread subjects and tags. Do not upload huge, misleading or unrelated files.

4. Moderation

Image threads and replies remain private until approved. Staff may reject or remove content that breaks these rules. Repeated or serious violations may lead to posting restrictions or account moderation.

Use Safety & Reports to report a concern. Do not retaliate or start public arguments about reports.

5. Your account and data

Keep your account secure. Do not share your password or use another member's account. Read the Privacy Center to understand what information the site stores and how to request an export or account deletion review.

6. Copyright and ownership

You keep ownership of artwork you create. By submitting an image, you give Sparky's Space permission to store and display that image on this site if approved. This permission is limited to operating the community gallery; it does not transfer ownership.

If you believe an image infringes your copyright, contact staff through the Safety & Reports page and include enough information to identify the work.

Questions?

These rules apply to the current community features. Additional rules may be added before new features, such as member messaging, are released.
