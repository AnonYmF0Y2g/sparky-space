Check the exact phone model

/e/OS supports specific device variants and installation methods. Confirm the exact model and codename in the official device list. A similarly named phone may use different hardware and require a different build.

Installation and data safety

Follow the official device-specific installation instructions. Unlocking the bootloader or replacing the operating system may erase data and affect security features. Back up files, contacts, messages, and authenticator access first.

App compatibility and privacy

Review the app catalogue and compatibility information before switching. Understand how location services, account synchronization, and optional compatibility components work. No mobile operating system can guarantee anonymity or prevent every form of tracking.

Updates and troubleshooting

Use the supported updater and read release notes. For a failed update, record the device codename, build, and exact message; do not flash firmware for another variant.

Troubleshooting and further help

Record the exact OS release, hardware model, error text, and changes made. Do not share passwords, recovery codes, or private keys.
