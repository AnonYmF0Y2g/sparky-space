13. Android phones and tablets

  • Install Android and app updates through the device's normal settings and official app store.
  • Open Settings → Battery → Battery usage to identify apps using unusually high power.
  • Review storage and remove downloads or apps you no longer need. Back up important photos and files before deleting them.
  • Restrict background activity only for apps where delayed notifications or syncing will not cause problems.
  • Restart the device if an app or service becomes stuck, and update or reinstall that app if the problem repeats.
  • Avoid “RAM booster,” “phone cleaner,” and task-killer apps that promise impossible speed gains. Android manages memory itself.
  • Do not root a phone or install unofficial firmware just to make it faster; that can weaken security and break updates.

14. iPhone and iPad optimization

  • Check Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage and review Apple's storage recommendations.
  • Offload unused apps when you want to free app storage while retaining their documents and data.
  • Check Settings → Battery for apps using more energy than expected.
  • Install supported iOS or iPadOS updates and keep enough free storage for the update process.
  • Use Low Power Mode when battery life matters more than background activity or some visual features.
  • Back up before major troubleshooting. Avoid unofficial “cleaner” apps and jailbreak tweaks advertised as performance fixes.
