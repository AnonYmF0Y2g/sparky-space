How support works

Open a private support ticket and continue the conversation here. Only you and authorized support staff can read your ticket. The page checks for replies automatically about every 30 seconds while it is open.

This is an asynchronous help desk, not a promise of instant live chat. Replies depend on staff availability. Please do not include passwords, login codes, recovery keys, payment details, home addresses, or other sensitive information. Staff will never need your password.

You need to sign in to open and track a private support ticket.
