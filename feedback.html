♡ Suggest an improvement

Members, staff, and guests can suggest reasonable improvements, request guides, report bugs, or share accessibility ideas. You do not need an account.

Please do not include passwords, login codes, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or other sensitive information. Feedback is visible to authorized staff for review. We do not ask for your email address.

You can submit as a guest. If you are signed in, your account will be associated with the feedback so staff can understand whether it came from a member or staff account.

What happens next?

  • Staff review suggestions and group similar requests.
  • Possible statuses include new, reviewing, planned, declined, and completed.
  • Submitting feedback does not guarantee that a feature will be added.
  • Guest submissions are rate-limited to help prevent spam. Signed-in accounts have a separate hourly limit.
