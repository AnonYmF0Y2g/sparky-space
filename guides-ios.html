Identify your device

Open Settings → General → About to record the model and software version. Check Apple's compatibility information before expecting a new release on an older device. Feature releases and security updates may cover different models.

Updates and permissions

Use Settings → General → Software Update. Review Settings → Privacy & Security for location, camera, microphone, photos, tracking, and Bluetooth access. App Privacy Report can help explain app access patterns. Prefer App Store apps and review developer information.

Storage and battery

Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage shows large apps and recommendations. Offload apps only after understanding which documents remain. Settings → Battery helps identify energy use. Battery health and service information varies by model.

Backups and recovery

Use iCloud Backup or a trusted computer backup. Confirm that it completed and that you can access your Apple Account before erasing or replacing a device. Some data is synced separately from backups. A recovery or erase process may remove local data.

Troubleshooting

App freezes: update the app and check storage. Battery drains: inspect battery usage and recent changes. Wi-Fi fails: compare another device and network. Device unavailable: follow Apple's official recovery process and understand whether it erases data.
