♡ What's New

A small changelog of improvements to Sparky's Space.

ImprovedAppearance

More ways to personalize

Theme and accent choices, text sizing, and reduced-motion preferences are available across the site.

Privacy

Member profile controls

Members can choose whether their profile appears publicly and whether their bio and status are shown. Public directory data is separated from private account records.

New

Service Status

A public page checks website assets, the directory API, and the authentication health endpoint from your browser.

New

Account Security

Signed-in members can review current-session details, request a password reset email, and sign out other sessions.

Community promise

Free to use, with no payments

Sparky's Space is free to use. We will never ask for payment, subscriptions, donations, or membership fees. The project is not operated for profit, and community feedback helps guide improvements.

New

IT Support Desk

Signed-in members can open private support tickets and follow replies. Staff can review and respond through the private Staff HQ. Replies are asynchronous and depend on staff availability.

Expanded

Device optimization and repair library

New guides cover Windows, Linux, macOS, Android, iPhone and iPad, Chromebooks, gaming performance, RAM, safe troubleshooting, and free manufacturer and community repair documentation.

New

Community Feedback

Guests and signed-in members can submit feature ideas, guide requests, bug reports, and accessibility suggestions. Staff can review submissions in the private Staff HQ.

New

Tech Guides Library

Practical guides explain DNS, VPNs, Tor Browser, browser privacy, Linux basics, and defensive OPSEC.

New

Optional Privacy Test Center

Visitors can opt into limited browser and IP checks. The site does not save test results, and external providers are disclosed before tests run.

Improved

Organized member profiles

Profile editing is grouped into sections, image uploads replace URL entry, members can remove their uploaded images, and public profile pages include verified staff-role badges, tags, badges, and guestbooks.

Existing

Community safety tools

Community reports, appeals, and staff moderation history remain available.

This page is updated when site changes are released. Planned ideas may change.
