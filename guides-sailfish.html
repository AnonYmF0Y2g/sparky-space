Check official device and region support

Sailfish OS availability, licensing, and features can vary by device and region. Check the official compatibility list and current release notes. Community ports may not provide the same support level as officially supported devices.

Updates and applications

Use the supported system updater and official app sources. Android application support depends on the device, release, and licensing. Confirm that essential apps and services work before switching your main phone.

Privacy and maintenance

Review application permissions, account synchronization, location access, and backup settings. Keep a separate backup of photos and documents. Read device-specific instructions before changing recovery or boot partitions.

Troubleshooting

Record device model, Sailfish release, whether it is an official or community port, and the exact error. Check the project knowledge base and device community before applying unofficial fixes.
