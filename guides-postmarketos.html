Mobile Linux with device-specific ports

postmarketOS brings Linux to phones and other mobile devices, but support varies by device and feature. A port may be marked as community, testing, or incomplete. Calls, cameras, suspend, GPU acceleration, and cellular data may not all work.

Check device status first

Use the official device database and wiki to inspect the exact model, codename, port status, and known limitations. Do not assume two phones from the same manufacturer share compatible images.

Installation and packages

Read the device-specific installation guide and understand partitioning, bootloader state, and recovery steps. Installation can erase data or make a device temporarily unusable. Use the supported Alpine-based package tools and repositories for the selected branch.

Learning and troubleshooting

Start with a spare device rather than a phone needed for emergency calls. Record the exact image, interface, kernel, and hardware state when reporting problems.

Troubleshooting and further help

Record the exact OS release, hardware model, error text, and changes made. Do not share passwords, recovery codes, or private keys.
