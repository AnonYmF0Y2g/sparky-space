♡ Privacy Center

This page explains what Sparky's Space uses, what is visible to other members, and what controls are currently available. It is based on a review of the website source and database access rules; it is not a legal certification or a live browser cookie scan.

What the site stores

  • Account: Supabase Auth manages your sign-in and account email. The browser stores the Supabase session so you can stay signed in.
  • Profile: username, display name, bio, status, avatar/banner links, accent, privacy settings, and timestamps.
  • Community safety: reports, appeals, moderation records, and limited security-event categories. Security events currently record event type, source category, and timestamp—not IP addresses.
  • Browser preferences: theme, accent, text size, and reduced-motion choice are stored locally under four sparky-* keys.

Who can see what

  • Private profile rows are readable by the account owner under database row-level security.
  • Only profiles explicitly published are copied into the public directory. Bio and status visibility are separate choices.
  • Members can view their own moderation records and submitted reports through the account's member-facing views.
  • Authorized staff roles can review reports, appeals, moderation records, and security events for community safety. The database enforces staff checks for these operations.
  • The current system does not record a complete audit trail of which staff member viewed each record.

External services

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and account session handling.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: bot checks on account and community report/appeal forms. Cloudflare may process browser/device signals and may use cookies as part of its service.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the Supabase JavaScript library used by the site.
  • The reviewed first-party source does not include advertising analytics or an analytics tracker. External providers have their own privacy practices.

The site itself does not request your location. This source review found no first-party code that creates cookies, but a complete cookie inventory requires testing actual browser responses and third-party behavior; this page does not claim that no cookies are ever set.

Manage this browser's preferences

Clear only the four appearance/accessibility preferences saved by this site. This does not sign you out or delete your account.

Export or delete your account

Sign in on the Account page to export your profile, submitted reports, moderation records about your account, and appeals. The JSON export is generated locally in your browser and excludes passwords and session tokens.

You can also submit an account deletion request from the Account page. A request is queued for staff review; it does not immediately delete your account. The website currently has no automatic account-deletion or data-retention scheduler.

Retention and improvements still in progress

Reports, appeals, moderation records, and security-event records do not yet have a published, automated retention schedule. They are not silently deleted by a timed cleanup job. A documented schedule and review process still need to be established before automatic cleanup is enabled.

Staff access is restricted by role-based database policies, but read-access auditing is not yet implemented. The site does not currently offer a full device/session history.

Questions about a privacy request can be raised with the site owner through the existing community support process. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, or private contact details in a public report.
