Device-specific support matters

LineageOS support is specific to exact device variants and maintainers. Check the official device list and codename before downloading anything. Unofficial builds have different risks and support expectations.

Installation and data safety

Read the device's official installation page from beginning to end. Requirements may include firmware versions, recovery environment, bootloader state, and a specific sequence. Unlocking a bootloader normally wipes data and changes device security properties. Back up files and authenticator access first.

Updates and troubleshooting

Use the updater and instructions for your exact device and build. Do not flash packages intended for another variant. If the device fails to boot after an update, stop repeating random steps; record the build, device codename, and last completed step and consult the official device page.
