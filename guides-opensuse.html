Leap and Tumbleweed are different

Leap follows a more fixed release model; Tumbleweed is a rolling distribution. Instructions for one may not apply to the other. Record the edition, release, desktop environment, and filesystem.

YaST and Zypper

YaST provides graphical and text-based system administration tools. Zypper manages packages. Review proposed package changes and repository priorities carefully. Avoid adding random repositories or mixing packages from incompatible openSUSE releases.

Snapshots and recovery

Many installations use Btrfs and Snapper snapshots. Snapshots can help roll back system changes, but they do not replace personal-file backups and may not cover every filesystem or configuration. Learn how your boot menu exposes snapshots before relying on them.

Troubleshooting

Dependency conflict: inspect repositories and package vendor changes. Upgrade issue: verify Leap/Tumbleweed instructions. Boot trouble: identify whether snapshots and the bootloader are configured as expected. Preserve logs and avoid forcing vendor changes without understanding their effect.

Troubleshooting and further help

Before asking for help, include the exact device model, operating-system version, error message, when the problem began, recent changes, and safe checks already completed. Do not share passwords, recovery codes, private keys, or personal documents.
